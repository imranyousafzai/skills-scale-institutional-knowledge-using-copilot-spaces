# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory is the central entry point for OctoAcme's project management processes and operating guidance. OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative, data-informed lifecycle that begins with initiation and continues through planning, execution, release, and retrospective learning. During initiation, teams validate the business problem, define success metrics, identify stakeholders, and decide whether to proceed into planning. Once approved, work is broken into prioritized increments with clear owners, estimates, milestones, acceptance criteria, and tracked dependencies.

During execution, work moves through a project board and team rituals such as daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, and stakeholder communication cycles. Project Managers coordinate schedule, risk, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; developers implement and test; QA validates acceptance; and stakeholders provide review and approval. The team also follows a structured pull request workflow that includes issue linkage, acceptance criteria, CI validation, and at least one approval before merge. Quality is reinforced through unit tests, integration tests, smoke tests, security scanning, and targeted manual QA for critical behaviors.

Release activities include readiness checks, deployment windows, staging validation, production verification, and communication to stakeholders and support teams. Risk and dependency management is handled through a shared risk register, escalation paths, and weekly review of blockers. After each sprint, milestone, or incident, OctoAcme uses retrospectives to capture what went well, what should improve, and which actions need follow-up. This creates a repeatable operating model that keeps teams aligned, reduces delivery risk, and makes continuous improvement part of the project lifecycle.
